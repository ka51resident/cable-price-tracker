How to read this

  • Rs/kg is the LME cash settlement price multiplied by the USD/INR rate. It leaves out import duty, premiums and the cost of turning cathode into rod, so the Indian rod price is always higher. Use it to measure change, not the absolute level. The duty + premium field scales it if you want.
  • Metal content uses nominal conductor area: 8.89 kg/km per sq mm for copper and 2.703 for aluminium. 3.5 core uses the standard reduced neutral. Screens, armour and insulation are not counted.
  • Gap is the new rate minus what the earlier rate would be after the metal move. Gaps under 4% are treated as noise. Compare like with like: same make, same construction.
  • If a date falls on a holiday or weekend, the last trading day before it is used.
  • LME is the benchmark Indian cable pricing follows, and the quote checker uses it. COMEX is the US futures market, shown for comparison only: it is the nearest-month contract, converted from US dollars per pound for copper, and it can sit well above or below LME because of US tariffs and freight. IEEMA is the Indian industry reference, published monthly.
